# Counterexamples

- Frozen encoder: trivially stable, but fails the nontrivial drift band.
- Fast-weight reset: avoids instability by discarding state instead of carrying it.
- Post-hoc noise injection: inflates drift without preserving predictive carryover.
- Decorative architecture bloat: adds modules without improving the inequality slack.
- Witness-only bluffing: reports attractive numbers without matching the required derivation tags.
